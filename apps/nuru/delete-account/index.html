Delete your account

You only have a NURU account if you signed in with Apple or Google to use Fasting Circles. Without one, everything NURU keeps is on your device and goes when you uninstall the app.

In the app

  1. Open NURU and go to Settings.
  2. Under Profile, tap Delete Account and confirm.

If NURU asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in, and delete again.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email support@fueera.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my NURU account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email address and account ID.
  • Your membership of every circle, and with it your name on those circles’ member lists.
  • Your push notification token.

What is kept

  • Purchase records that Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep for tax and accounting, for as long as the law requires.
  • Circle activity already shared with other members before you left, such as a circle’s overall streak.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Circles Premium subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (Settings › your name › Subscriptions) or Google Play (Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions).

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
