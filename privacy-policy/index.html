Privacy Policy

Fueera LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use any of our mobile applications (including EOTC Calendar, Ethiopian Calendar & Widgets, NURU — Digital Fasting, and DuoBaby), as well as our website at apps.fueera.com (collectively, the "Services").

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us: We may collect information you provide directly to us when you contact customer support, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

Automatically collected information: Our apps may collect anonymous usage data (like crash reports or device types) to help us improve performance and stability. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless explicitly requested and provided by you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Services
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests
  • Monitor and analyze app performance, usage, and trends

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services (such as Google AdMob for advertising or Firebase for crash reporting). These third-party services may collect information used to identify you, such as your IP address or device identifier. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party providers for more details.

4. Digital Fasting in EOTC Calendar (Android)

If you turn on Digital Fasting in EOTC Calendar for Android, the app uses Android's Usage access permission to see which app is currently open and the Display over other apps permission to show the fasting screen. The apps you choose and your app usage information are processed only on your device. They are not collected, stored on our servers, or shared with anyone. You can turn Digital Fasting and these permissions off at any time.

5. NURU — Digital Fasting

Without an account, NURU keeps everything on your device: your fasts, the apps you choose to block, your streaks, and your phone usage history. On Android, NURU uses the Accessibility service only to notice which app is in front so it can cover an app you chose to block during a fast, and Usage access to show your screen time; neither reads your screen content, what you type, or your messages, and none of it leaves your device.

With an account (Sign in with Apple or Google, needed for Fasting Circles), we store your name, email address and account ID in Google Firebase, together with the circles you join and what each circle needs to share with its members: your display name, whether you are fasting, the breaks you take and your circle streak. We also store a push notification token so circles can notify you. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; RevenueCat records your purchase status against your account ID so Circles Premium works on every device you sign in on.

Deleting your account: in NURU, open Settings › Profile › Delete Account. This leaves your circles and deletes your profile and account. You can also ask us by email — see Delete your NURU account. Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting an account; cancel them in the App Store or Google Play.

6. Children's Privacy

Some of our applications, such as DuoBaby, are designed for early childhood development. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and our children's apps are designed to comply with applicable guidelines, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@fueera.com
