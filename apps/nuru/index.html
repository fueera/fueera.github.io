The pause is the point.

Every early unlock goes through the same loop. Hover or tap a blocked app to walk through it.

  3. 03 Reflect. A question to sit with while you wait.
  4. 04 Return. Most of the time, you go back to what matters.

In the app the pause lasts five minutes. This demo runs for twelve seconds.
